# Agriculture

**Summary**: Notes on agricultural technology, remote-sensing-driven farm analytics, and related datasets/tools.
**Last updated**: 2026-08-24.

---

- [agribound](https://github.com/montimaj/agribound): Python package for delineating field boundaries. Combines object detection, semantic segmentation, vision transformers, foundation-model embeddings, and multi-engine ensembling to delineate agricultural field boundaries from satellite composites, automatically filtering out non-agricultural polygons using land cover data. Keywords: field boundaries, agriculture, satellite imagery, foundation models, segmentation, GIS. See also [[Remote Sensing]], [[Python]], [[Embeddings]].
